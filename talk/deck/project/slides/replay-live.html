How it works

Checkpoint and replay, in motion

2:30 to 3:45. No clicks: this runs the function from the previous slide as a motion graphic that loops every 18 seconds. A glowing runner is the invocation. First run: it reaches step-1, the step runs, and an orange checkpoint flies into the log on the right; the same at wait-10s. Then the runner disappears, the track dims, the compute bar empties and a ring counts the wait down. Invocation 2 starts from the left in orange: as it passes each completed step a beam comes back from the log and the step is marked replayed without running. Past the wait it turns blue again, runs the new code and the execution succeeds. Narrate over one full loop. A click-through diagram of the same story is kept as a hidden slide just before this one, as a fallback for a PDF export. It plays in the web deck; a PDF or PowerPoint export shows a still frame.
